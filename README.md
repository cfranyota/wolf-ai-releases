# Wolf AI

Live call coaching for real estate agents, for Mac. Wolf AI listens to your calls, follows your script, and suggests what to say next.

**Free beta.** This repository holds the downloads only.

## Download

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/cfranyota/wolf-ai-releases/releases/latest)**

Pick the DMG for your Mac (Apple menu › About This Mac):

- **Apple Silicon** (Chip: Apple M1, M2, M3 or M4): `Wolf-AI-vX.Y.Z-mac-arm64.dmg`
- **Intel** (Processor: Intel): `Wolf-AI-vX.Y.Z-mac-x64.dmg`

## Install

1. Open the DMG and drag **Wolf AI** into **Applications**. If you have an older version, replace it.
2. Open Wolf AI from Applications. macOS says it can't verify the app, because this build isn't notarized by Apple. Click **Done**.
3. Open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll down, click **Open Anyway** next to the Wolf AI message, and confirm.

## Set up

- Open the **…** menu › **Settings**: fill in **Your profile**, add your **Deepgram API key** (live transcription), then pick an **AI Engine** (Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, Groq, Gemini, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, Ollama or any OpenAI-compatible service) and add its key.
- Allow **Microphone** when macOS asks.
- To hear the other side of the call, turn on **System audio** (needs macOS 14.2 or later). Switch Wolf AI on under **Screen & System Audio Recording** when asked, then reopen Wolf AI.

Each release lists what's new and includes `SHA256SUMS.txt` with the checksums of both DMGs.
